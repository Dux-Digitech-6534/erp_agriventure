How to install

1
Tap Download & Install

The APK saves to your phone. Open it from the notification or your Downloads.

2
Allow this source

If Android warns, tap Settings → allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser, then go back.

3
Sign in

Open Agriventure and log in with your usual email & password — your roles and access come with you.
